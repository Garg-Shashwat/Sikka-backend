Sikka Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

In short: Sikka stores the expenses you record so you can see them on your devices and share them with people you choose. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Sikka is an expense tracking app made by Shashwat Garg (“we”). Questions or requests: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider) and the name you set.
  • What you record: expenses, settlements, transfers and refunds (amounts, dates, descriptions, notes), the people and groups you add (names, and phone numbers if you enter them), and your categories and tags.
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you add a receipt.
  • Connections: email addresses you send connection requests to, invite codes, and who is connected with whom.
  • Edit history of shared expenses: who changed what and when.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability (for example IP address and time of requests).

The people you add (for example “Mom”) are names in your own records; Sikka doesn't contact them.

How we use it

  • To run the app: store your records, sync them across your devices and work out who owes whom.
  • To share expenses with people you connect with, when you choose to.
  • To send sign-up and password-reset codes by email.
  • To keep the service secure: limiting abuse and controlling who can sign up.

Who can see your data

  • Only you, for anything you don't share: personal expenses, your categories and tags.
  • People you connect with, for what you share with them: expenses you're both part of, groups you're both in, the names of the people and categories on those, their notes and receipts, and their edit history. Connecting always needs both sides to agree, and either side can disconnect.
  • Service providers that run Sikka for us, only to provide the service: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and Google (sending sign-in emails). They don't use your data for their own purposes.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on our provider's servers. Access is controlled on the server, so each account can read only its own data and what others have shared with it. A copy of your data is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account and everything you recorded straight away (see how to delete your account). Expenses other people recorded with you stay in their accounts, showing you only as a plain name. Provider security logs are kept for a short period and then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data in the app at any time.
  • Delete individual records, disconnect from people, or delete your whole account in the app.
  • Ask us about your data, or for a copy of it, at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

If you are in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; contact us at the address above for any grievance.

Children

Sikka is not meant for children under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
