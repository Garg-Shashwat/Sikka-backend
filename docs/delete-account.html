Delete your Sikka account

Sikka is an expense tracking app by Shashwat Garg.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Sikka and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't use the app?

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL from the email address you use for Sikka, with the subject “Delete my Sikka account”. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login (email and password).
  • Your profile name.
  • Everything you recorded: expenses, settlements, refunds, people, groups, categories, tags and notes.
  • Receipt photos you added.
  • Your connections to other Sikka users.

What is kept

  • Expenses that other people recorded with you stay in their own accounts, with you shown only as a plain name (for example “Asha”). Your email and account are no longer linked to them.
  • Nothing else is kept. Sikka doesn't keep backups of deleted accounts.
